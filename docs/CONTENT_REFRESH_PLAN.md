## TONE GUIDELINES (Contemporary/Creative - Artist Voice)

### DO:
- Use present tense, active voice
- Reference "we author," "we create," "we explore"
- Poetic metaphors: "portals," "worlds," "journeys," "transformations"
- Specific cultural references (not generic "immersive")
- Philosophical depth: Why this work matters culturally
- Human creativity emphasis: "Informed by [community]," "Collaborated with [practitioners]"

### DON'T:
- Use AI/tech jargon: No "generated," "machine learning," "algorithms"
- Generic corporate speak: Avoid "cutting-edge," "innovative" without context
- Product language: Avoid "product," "service," "offering" — use "work," "experience," "collaboration"
- Oversell technology: It's a vehicle for art, not the art itself
- Cultural appropriation: Always credit, acknowledge, collaborate
